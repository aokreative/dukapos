# "How to use Duka POS" — Video Script

**Length:** ~2 min 45 sec · **Style:** warm, upbeat, Kenyan everyday-life
**Voiceover language:** simple English with light Kiswahili flavour
**Music:** modern afro-pop instrumental, light percussion, builds gently

**Production note:** Scenes marked 🎬 GENERATE are for the AI video generator
(lifestyle/b-roll). Scenes marked 📱 SCREEN are recordings of the real app —
record these on a phone (screen record while using the app; demo data is
already loaded). Mixing real screen recordings with generated b-roll makes the
video believable AND beautiful.

---

## SCENE 1 — The problem (0:00–0:20)

🎬 GENERATE: A busy Kenyan corner shop (duka) in warm morning light. A
friendly shopkeeper serves a queue — sodas, bread, airtime. She scribbles a
credit sale into a tattered exercise book. Close-up: pages of crossed-out
names and smudged numbers.

**VO:**
> "You work hard. You sell all day. But at the end of the month… where did
> the money go? Who took goods on credit and never came back? That old
> notebook is not protecting your business."

**On-screen text:** *Kenyan shops lose thousands every month in forgotten mkopo.*

---

## SCENE 2 — Meet Duka POS (0:20–0:35)

🎬 GENERATE: The same shopkeeper picks up her smartphone and smiles. Camera
pushes in on the phone in her hand. (The screen itself will be replaced with
a real app recording in editing.)

📱 SCREEN: The Duka POS home screen — the green till with the product grid.

**VO:**
> "Meet Duka POS. The simple app that runs your shop — on the phone you
> already have. No new machines. No training. If you can use WhatsApp, you
> can use Duka POS."

**On-screen text:** *Duka POS — Sell · Track · Get Paid*

---

## SCENE 3 — Sell in seconds (0:35–0:55)

📱 SCREEN: Finger taps "Sugar 1kg", then "Milk 500ml". Cart totals update
instantly. Tap **Charge** → tap **Exact cash**. The receipt pops up.
Then: tap **M-PESA** on another sale, type the code, done.

**VO:**
> "Selling takes seconds. Tap the product, take the money — cash, M-PESA,
> Airtel Money, even split payments. Every sale is saved, every receipt is
> ready — print it or send it straight to WhatsApp."

**On-screen text:** *Cash · M-PESA · Airtel Money · Card · Mkopo*

---

## SCENE 4 — The killer feature: mkopo that pays you back (0:55–1:30)

📱 SCREEN: A customer buys on **Credit** — the app asks for the customer,
"John Otieno" is picked. Cut to the **Debts** screen: John's name, KES 3,500,
"12 days old". Finger taps **Remind → WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the
message ALREADY WRITTEN — amount, receipt number, and the shop's M-PESA till
number inside. Send. ✓✓

🎬 GENERATE (quick cut): A young man at a bus stage reads the WhatsApp
message, nods, and dials M-PESA on his phone.

📱 SCREEN: Back in Duka POS — the debt is marked paid, balance KES 0,
a small "settled ✓" state.

**VO:**
> "And here is the magic. Every credit sale is remembered — who, how much,
> for how long. One tap sends a polite WhatsApp or SMS reminder… with YOUR
> till number already inside, so paying you back is easy. You can even let
> Duka remind them automatically. This is the app that collects your money
> for you."

**On-screen text:** *Automatic reminders · Your till number included · Debts get paid*

---

## SCENE 5 — Know your business + Duka AI (1:30–1:55)

📱 SCREEN: The **Reports** page — today's sales, the 7-day chart, top
products. Then the **Duka AI** tab: typing "Who owes me money?" — the answer
appears listing debtors and amounts. Then "What should I restock?" — instant
answer.

**VO:**
> "At any moment, ask your shop anything. 'How are sales today?' 'Who owes me
> money?' 'What should I restock?' Duka AI answers from your own numbers —
> like having an accountant inside the till."

**On-screen text:** *Duka AI — your shop, answered.*

---

## SCENE 6 — Works offline, works everywhere (1:55–2:15)

🎬 GENERATE: Rain over a small town, a power cut — the shop goes dim, but the
shopkeeper keeps serving customers by phone light, unbothered.

📱 SCREEN: Airplane mode ON at the top of the phone — a sale still completes
perfectly.

**VO:**
> "No internet? No problem. Duka POS works completely offline — every sale
> safe on your phone, syncing to the cloud when you're back online. Phone,
> tablet or computer — your whole shop, on every device, always in step."

**On-screen text:** *Offline-first · Multi-device sync · Staff PINs*

---

## SCENE 7 — Call to action (2:15–2:45)

🎬 GENERATE: Golden-hour shot — the shopkeeper closes the shop smiling,
checks her phone one last time: the day's total on the Reports screen.

**VO:**
> "Thousands of shillings recovered. Hours of stress gone. Duka POS starts at
> just one thousand nine hundred shillings a month — with fourteen days
> completely free. Your duka deserves better than a notebook. Karibu Duka POS."

**On-screen text (big, final card):**
*Duka POS — Start your FREE 14-day trial today*
*[your website] · WhatsApp [your number]*
