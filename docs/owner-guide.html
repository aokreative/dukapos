Part 1

Understand your business

You are the platform owner. Shops (dukas) use your POS to sell, and pay you a monthly fee to use it.

There are two pieces, and both are already built for you:

  • The POS app — what each shop uses to sell, track who owes them (debt), and send WhatsApp/SMS reminders. Works on any phone, tablet or computer, even offline.
  • The biller (helper service) — your money machine. It charges each shop every month by M-PESA, and if a shop doesn't pay, it automatically locks their POS until they do.
How you make money: every shop pays you monthly (from KES 1,900 to KES 24,900 depending on their size). The money lands in your M-PESA Till/Paybill. You never chase anyone — the system charges them and enforces payment by holding the POS.
Part 2 · about 1 hour · free

Put the system online

Two free sign-ups: one for the app (Vercel), one for the biller (Render). Your code is already on GitHub — you're just switching it on.

2A · The POS app (Vercel) — ~10 min

  1. Create a free account Go to vercel.com → Sign Up → Continue with GitHub. Approve when GitHub asks.
  2. Import the project Add New… → Project → find duka-pos → Import → Deploy. Wait ~2 minutes for "Congratulations".
  3. Get your link You'll get an address like https://duka-pos-xxxx.vercel.app. This is the POS. Bookmark it.

2B · The biller (Render) — ~10 min

  1. Create a free account Go to render.com → Sign up → Continue with GitHub.
  2. Create from blueprint New + → Blueprint → choose duka-pos → Apply. You'll get an address like https://duka-pos-backend.onrender.com. Copy it.
  3. Connect the app to the biller In Vercel → your project → Settings → Environment Variables, add: name VITE_API_URL, value = your Render address. Then Deployments → Redeploy.

2C · Give shops a memory that can't be wiped (database)

By default the biller remembers shops in a simple file. For real business, use a free database so nothing is ever lost:

  1. Create a free database Go to supabase.com → new project. Open Project Settings → Database → Connection string (URI) and copy it.
  2. Give it to the biller In Render → your service → Environment, add DATABASE_URL = that connection string. Render restarts and creates the table by itself.
How to know it worked
Open https://YOUR-backend.onrender.com/api/health in a browser. It should say "storage":"postgres" once the database is connected (or "memory" before that).
Part 3 · apply once · approval takes days

Connect M-PESA & messages

These let you take real money and send real WhatsApp/SMS. Start the M-PESA application early — approval is the slowest part. Until keys are added, everything runs in a safe demo mode.

3A · M-PESA (to collect subscription money)

  • You need a Lipa na M-PESA Till or Paybill (from Safaricom / M-PESA Business) — this is where shops' payments land.
  • Then register on the Safaricom Daraja developer portal (developer.safaricom.co.ke) and create an app to get your keys.
Paste these into Render → Environment
MPESA_CONSUMER_KEY   MPESA_CONSUMER_SECRET   MPESA_SHORTCODE (your Till/Paybill)   MPESA_PASSKEY   MPESA_ENV=production

3B · Messages (so reminders send by themselves)

To turn onSign up atPaste into Render
Automatic SMSafricastalking.comAT_API_KEY, AT_USERNAME
Automatic WhatsAppbusiness.facebook.comWHATSAPP_TOKEN, WHATSAPP_PHONE_ID
Order of operations
You can go live and start selling before messages are approved — shops just tap "Remind" by hand (still one tap, still includes your pay details). Turn on automatic sending whenever the keys arrive.
Where each value goes
App settings (like VITE_API_URL) go in Vercel. Money/message keys (like MPESA_*) go in Render. That's the only rule to remember.

Every shop starts on a 14-day free trial with full features — no money needed to begin.

Want to change a price?

Prices live in one small file. Ask your developer (or a follow-up session) to edit src/lib/plans.ts (the app) and server/lib/plans.js (the biller) — change the price numbers, save, and it redeploys. Keep the two files matching.

Pricing tip for your waiting clients
Offer the annual plan to anyone who can pay up front — you get 10 months of cash today and they save 2 months. For the rest, monthly on auto-charge keeps cash flowing without you chasing.
Part 5

How shops are charged automatically

M-PESA can't silently take money like a card — so "automatic" works in one of four ways. All are built in; you choose which to offer.

MethodHow automaticWhat the shop does
1. Auto STK push on the due dateSemi — you charge, they approveEnters M-PESA PIN when the prompt pops up
2. Paybill auto-detectAutomatic detectionPays your Paybill like any bill; system renews them
3. M-PESA Ratiba (standing order)Fully automaticApproves once; Safaricom debits every month by itself
4. Card (optional)Fully automaticEnters card once
The part that makes shops actually pay: if a shop is late, the POS holds — day 1–7 it still works (with reminders), day 8–14 it can view but can't sell, day 15+ it's locked with a "Pay to reactivate" screen. The moment they pay, it unlocks. This is automatic and already built.

What you do

  • Leave the biller's scheduler on (it is by default) — every due shop gets charged automatically each cycle.
  • For the most hands-off shops, offer Ratiba at sign-up so it debits with zero taps.
  • You can watch it happen safely in demo mode before going live (see the "Try it" box in BILLING.md).
Recommended setup
Ratiba for shops who accept it → STK push on the due date for everyone else → Paybill auto-detect as a safety net → the POS-hold to enforce it. That combination is automatic for most and self-enforcing for the rest.
Part 6

Sign up a new shop

Do this with each client. It takes about 15 minutes and they can sell the same day.

  1. Send them the link Give them your Vercel POS link. On their phone they open it in Chrome/Safari and choose Add to Home Screen — now it's an app icon that works offline.
  2. Set up their shop In Settings: shop name, phone number (this is their billing identity), location, and their own M-PESA Till/Paybill (so their customers can pay them — separate from your platform Till).
  3. Pick their plan On the Billing screen, use "Find your fit" (enter their shops/staff/products) — it recommends Starter, Standard, Growth or Chain. Their 14-day free trial starts automatically.
  4. Add their products In Stock, add items (name, price, stock). Then Settings → Data → Clear everything once to remove the demo items and start clean.
  5. Show them the essentials Selling on the Sell screen, recording credit (mkopo) under a customer, and tapping Remind on the Debts screen to WhatsApp/SMS a debtor.
  6. They're live On day 10 of the trial they'll be nudged to pay; on day 14 the trial ends and the auto-charge begins. Nothing more for you to do.
Their phone number matters
The number you enter in a shop's Settings is how the biller identifies and charges them. Use the owner's real M-PESA number.
Part 7

Selling it to your clients

You already have shops waiting. Here's a simple way to pitch and close.

The 20-second pitch

"It's a POS that works even when your internet is down, and it automatically reminds customers who owe you — by WhatsApp and SMS — with your M-PESA number in the message so they can pay on the spot. You get paid faster. It's KES 1,900 to KES 4,900 a month, and the first 14 days are free."

The three things shop owners care about

  • "Will it work when Safaricom is down?" Yes — it keeps selling offline and syncs later.
  • "Can it help me collect debts (mkopo)?" Yes — it tracks who owes and sends reminders with your pay details. This alone usually pays for the subscription.
  • "Is it hard?" No — a sale takes seconds, and it's on their phone.

Handling "it's too expensive"

  • Point at the debt feature: recovering even one or two forgotten debts a month covers the fee.
  • Offer the Starter plan (KES 1,900) to very small shops.
  • Offer annual — they pay for 10 months, use 12.
  • Let the 14-day free trial do the selling — they try it risk-free.
A smooth first week
Sign up 2–3 shops, sit with each for 15 minutes, and make sure they process one real sale and send one real reminder while you're there. Happy early users bring you the next ones.
Part 8

Running it day to day

  • Watch your shops: open https://YOUR-backend.onrender.com/api/admin/tenants to see every shop, their plan, and whether they're paid, in grace, on hold, or suspended.
  • When a shop doesn't pay: do nothing — the system moves them from grace → hold → suspended and keeps prompting them to pay. They reactivate instantly when they do.
  • When a shop pays: nothing to do — their POS unlocks and an invoice is recorded automatically.
  • New client: follow Part 6. Price change: see Part 4.
Two things to keep healthy
  • The free Render biller "sleeps" when idle. For punctual monthly charging, upgrade Render to a small paid tier (a few dollars/month) so it's always awake.
  • Keep your M-PESA and database keys safe. Never share them or post them anywhere.

Part 9

Staff logins & your control room

Two kinds of access: staff inside each shop, and you across all shops.

9A · Staff on the shop device

Each shop's device is PIN-locked. Staff tap their name and enter a PIN to sign in; the lock button switches user. The owner adds staff in Settings → Staff & roles.

RoleCan doCannot do
OwnerEverything—
ManagerSell, stock, reports, discounts, debtsBilling, manage staff
CashierSell & serve customers, view/chase debtsEdit stock/prices, reports, settings, discounts
Setting PINs
Give each person their own PIN and role. Demo PINs to start: Owner 1234, Cashier 0000 — change these before real use (Settings → Staff & roles).

9B · Your Super-Admin portal (all shops)

You get a private control room to run the whole business:

  1. Set your admin password In Render → your service → Environment, add ADMIN_TOKEN = a long secret you make up.
  2. Open your portal Go to https://YOUR-backend.onrender.com/admin and paste that token.
  3. Run the business See every shop with its plan, status and renewal date, your estimated monthly revenue (MRR), and buttons to charge a shop now or run a billing sweep.
Keep this token secret
Anyone with the admin token can see and manage all your shops. Don't share it. If it leaks, change ADMIN_TOKEN in Render.
If the app itself has a problem
You own the code and the servers, so any issue can be fixed and redeployed — by you with help, or in a follow-up session. Your shops' data is safe in your database (Part 2C).
