Questions

Asked all the time

What is the best POS system for a small shop in Kenya?

Duka POS is a simple, offline-first point-of-sale built specifically for Kenyan shops. It runs on any phone, tablet or computer, keeps selling when the internet is down, tracks mkopo (credit) debts, sends WhatsApp and SMS reminders with your M-PESA till number included, prompts customers to pay by M-PESA STK, and is KRA eTIMS-ready — from KES 1,900/month with a 14-day free trial.

Is Duka POS a good alternative to Loyverse or Square in Kenya?

Yes. Global systems like Loyverse and Square are excellent, but they aren't built around Kenyan realities — M-PESA STK payments, KRA eTIMS compliance, mkopo debt reminders and true offline selling. Duka POS is built for those from the ground up, in one app, priced in shillings from KES 1,900/month.

Do I need internet to sell?

No. Duka POS is offline-first — selling, debts and receipts all work with zero connection. The internet is only needed for WhatsApp/SMS sending, cloud sync and updates.

How do the debt reminders work?

Each credit customer has a balance. Tap "Remind" and Duka opens WhatsApp or SMS with a polite message already written — including the amount and YOUR M-PESA till/Paybill (or Airtel number), so the customer can pay immediately. You can also switch on automatic reminders on a schedule you choose.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

You pay for your plan via M-PESA inside the app (or set up auto-pay). If payment lapses, the POS politely holds until it's sorted — your data is never deleted.

Which devices does it run on?

Any phone, tablet, laptop or desktop with a browser. It installs to the home screen like a normal app — Android, iPhone and Windows all work.

Is my data safe?

Your data lives on your device and — with cloud sync on — in your own secure cloud account that only your shop's login can access. Staff PINs keep cashiers out of your settings and profit numbers.

Can it do KRA eTIMS?

Yes. Turn it on in Settings to print your KRA PIN on receipts; with eTIMS onboarding completed, sales are submitted to KRA automatically.
